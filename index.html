The Vision

One brotherhood. One direction. The Power of Us.

Akatakyie USA is at its strongest when every year group, every chapter and every member can see themselves in the work. This campaign is about turning that goodwill into structure — a union that is organised, transparent and financially sound enough to serve OWASS and its sons for generations to come.

Ready means we do not spend the first year learning the job. Proven means the commitments made here are the same kind of commitments that have already been kept. That is the standard this leadership is offering.

What this presidency stands on

The Plan

The first hundred days

A presidency should be judged on what it finishes, not what it announces. These are the commitments the campaign is prepared to be measured against.

  1. 01

    Open the books

    Publish a full statement of the union's finances and a plain-language annual budget every member can read.

  2. 02

    Rebuild the register

    One verified national membership roll, by year group and chapter, so we know exactly who we are and who we are missing.

  3. 03

    Anchor the endowment

    Formalise the endowment with written investment rules, a stewardship committee and quarterly reporting.

  4. 04

    Chapters first

    Visit and resource every active chapter, and help dormant ones restart with a template that actually works.

  5. 05

    Back the school

    Agree one flagship OWASS project with the school's leadership — scoped, funded and delivered within the term.

  6. 06

    Bring in the young ones

    A mentorship and career network pairing recent leavers with members already established in their field.
